Agentic AI:
Personal and Enterprise

led by Kerry Back

This course is about working with agentic AI on your own tasks and putting it to work inside an organization. It opens with the working parts of an agent — sandboxing, MCP connectors, system prompts, skills, and plugins. It then looks at how a large language model works: why it is good at writing code and fails at judgment, how to prompt it well, and how to verify what it produces. Week 3 turns to what AI produces: Office documents, HTML and Markdown, and vibe-coded software that automates workflows. Week 4 customizes AI: skills that tailor generic agents, custom agents, and the security issues they raise. Week 5 applies all of this: personal assistants, data retrieval and analysis, deploying software, and sharing plugins in a marketplace. Week 6 covers additional topics: open-weight models, running models locally, retrieval-augmented generation, and adding voice to your agent.
